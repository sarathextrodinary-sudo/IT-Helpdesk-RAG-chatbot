SECTION 1: ACCESS TO COMPANY SHARED DRIVES & CLOUD STORAGE
================================================================================
1.1 Overview
Company files are stored across two primary environments:
- On-Premise Network Shared Drives (S:\ Department Drive, P:\ Project Drive)
- Cloud Storage (Microsoft SharePoint & OneDrive for Business)

1.2 Prerequisites for Network Shared Drives (S:\ and P:\)
- You must be connected to the corporate office Wi-Fi (Corp-Secure) or connected remotely via GlobalProtect VPN.
- Your Active Directory (AD) account must be added to the specific department security group.

1.3 How to Request Shared Drive Access
Step 1: Log in to the IT Service Portal (https://itportal.corp.internal).
Step 2: Navigate to Service Catalog > Identity & Access Management > Shared Folder Access.
Step 3: Select the Drive Letter (S:\ or P:\) and enter the exact folder path (e.g., \\fileserver01\Finance\2026_Reports).
Step 4: Select access level: "Read-Only" or "Read/Write (Modify)".
Step 5: Enter your Reporting Manager's email address for automated approval.
SLA: Once approved by your manager, access is provisioned automatically within 2 hours. Restart your computer or log off/on to refresh your security token.

1.4 Mapping a Network Drive Manually (Windows 11)
Step 1: Open File Explorer (Win + E) and click "This PC" on the left panel.
Step 2: Click the three dots (...) on the top toolbar and select "Map network drive".
Step 3: Choose Drive Letter "S:" from the dropdown.
Step 4: In the Folder box, type: \\corp-fs01.internal\Shared
Step 5: Check "Reconnect at sign-in" and click "Finish".

1.5 Troubleshooting Shared Drive Issues
- Error: "Network path was not found" -> Ensure GlobalProtect VPN is connected. If connected, disconnect and reconnect VPN to refresh DNS.
- Error: "Access is denied" -> Your security group sync may still be pending, or you lack permissions for a restricted subfolder. Raise an IT ticket with a screenshot of the exact path.

SECTION 2: TROUBLESHOOTING MICROSOFT OUTLOOK & CORPORATE EMAIL
================================================================================
2.1 Common Symptoms
- Outlook stuck on "Loading Profile" or "Trying to connect..."
- Password prompt keeps popping up repeatedly even after entering the correct password.
- Emails are stuck in the Outbox or new emails are not arriving.

2.2 Step-by-Step Self-Service Fixes (Perform in Order)
Step 1: Check Internet & VPN Status
- Verify you have active internet access by opening a browser.
- Note: Outlook uses Modern Authentication and does NOT require VPN to work, unless you are accessing legacy on-premise shared mailboxes.

Step 2: Check "Work Offline" Mode
- Open Outlook, go to the "Send / Receive" tab at the top.
- Look at the "Work Offline" button. If it is highlighted/shaded, click it once to switch back to Online mode. Look at the bottom right status bar; it should say "Connected to: Microsoft Exchange".

Step 3: Clear Cached Credentials (Fixes Password Loop)
- Close Outlook completely.
- Press Windows Key, type "Credential Manager", and press Enter.
- Click on "Windows Credentials".
- Locate any entries starting with "MicrosoftOffice16" or "msteams" or your corporate email ID.
- Click the arrow next to each and select "Remove".
- Restart your laptop and open Outlook; sign in with your corporate email and complete MFA (Microsoft Authenticator).

Step 4: Repair Outlook Data File / Recreate Profile
- Close Outlook.
- Open Control Panel > Mail (Microsoft Outlook) > Show Profiles.
- Click "Add...", name the new profile "CorpMail_New", and follow the Auto Account Setup wizard using your corporate email address.
- Select "Always use this profile" and choose "CorpMail_New" from the dropdown, then click OK.

2.3 Webmail Backup Access
If the Outlook desktop app is down and you need urgent email access:
- Open Chrome or Edge and go to: https://outlook.office.com
- Sign in using your full corporate email address (firstname.lastname@company.com) and network password.

SECTION 3: CHECKING COMPANY LAPTOP WARRANTY & HARDWARE LIFECYCLE
================================================================================
3.1 Standard Hardware Refresh Policy
- Standard corporate laptops (Dell Latitude, Lenovo ThinkPad, Apple MacBook Pro) carry a 3-year enterprise ProSupport / AppleCare+ warranty.
- Laptops older than 36 months (3 years) from the asset assignment date are eligible for a standard hardware refresh.

3.2 How to Find Your Laptop Serial Number / Service Tag
- Windows (Command Prompt Method):
  1. Press Win + R, type "cmd", and hit Enter.
  2. Type the following command and press Enter:
     wmic bios get serialnumber
  3. Note down the 7-character Service Tag (Dell) or 8-character Serial Number (Lenovo).
- MacBook:
  1. Click the Apple Icon (top-left) > "About This Mac".
  2. Copy the "Serial Number" listed at the bottom.
- Physical Sticker: Check the bottom metal chassis of the laptop for a barcode labeled "S/N" or "ST".

3.3 How to Check Warranty Status Online
- Option A: Internal Asset Portal (Recommended)
  1. Go to https://itportal.corp.internal > My Assets.
  2. View your assigned laptop model, Asset Tag (e.g., CORP-LT-8842), purchase date, and exact warranty expiration date.
- Option B: Vendor Warranty Lookup
  - Dell Laptops: Visit https://www.dell.com/support/home and enter your 7-character Service Tag.
  - Lenovo Laptops: Visit https://pcsupport.lenovo.com and enter your Serial Number.
  - Apple MacBooks: Visit https://checkcoverage.apple.com and enter your Serial Number.

3.4 What to Do If Your Laptop is Out of Warranty or Damaged
- Accidental Damage (cracked screen, liquid spill): Covered under our corporate Accidental Damage Protection (ADP). Raise a "Hardware Repair" ticket immediately; do not take corporate laptops to third-party local repair shops.
- Expired Warranty (Over 3 years old): Raise a "Laptop Refresh Request" under Hardware Services. Manager approval is not required if the asset is >36 months old.
